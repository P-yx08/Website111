Why should we drink enough water?

A study of CDC shows, drinking enough water can prevent diseases, build up good moods, help reduce calorie intake.

How much sleep do teenagers need?

According to a recommend of American Academy of Sleep medicine, teenagers should have about 8 to 10 hours of sleep each night.

How to keep a balance diet?

An evidence from WHO shows the health benefits of a diet high in whole grains, vegetables, fruit, legumes and nuts, and low in salt, free sugars and fats, particularly saturated and trans fats.

What are keys to keep exercise?

Based on Scripps article, If start exercise seems hard, just start from a small step.Pick exercises and activities you like. Try to make movement a normal part of your daily routine.
